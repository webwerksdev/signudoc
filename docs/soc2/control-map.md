# Control map

The SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria (2017, revised points of focus 2022), and for each one what a SignuDoc install gives you, what your vendors give you, and what is left for your organization. "Yours" items are where an auditor will ask for your own evidence.

Most SignuDoc installs put **Security** (the common criteria, CC) in scope, and often **Availability** and **Confidentiality**. **Processing Integrity** and **Privacy** are covered at the end.

Where a row says "evidence", it names what you can show an auditor.

## P Privacy

SOC 2 privacy (P1 to P8) follows your privacy notice. SignuDoc helps with: a consent to sign electronically before signing, an account export window before deletion, retention rules, and deletion of closed accounts. Data subject requests (access, correction, deletion) are handled by an administrator with the database or the app; there is no self-service request form. See [docs/self-hosting.md](../self-hosting.md#running-in-the-eu) for running in the EU.

## Known gaps

What an auditor may raise, and what to do:

| Gap                                                                                       | What to do                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Someone with full database access can rewrite an audit chain consistently.                | Limit Neon console access, and keep audit trails outside the database: the SIEM copy, or monthly CSVs. |
| No SAML (OIDC only).                                                                      | Okta, Entra ID and Google all offer OIDC for the same application.                                     |
| No SCIM provisioning. A person removed in your identity provider keeps their account row. | They cannot sign in again with SSO and password sign-in off. Review accounts under People quarterly.   |
| No IP allow list in the app.                                                              | Vercel Firewall rules, on a Vercel plan that has them.                                                 |
| Signers outside your organization sign through an emailed link.                           | Turn on the signer check (emailed code or access code) for documents that need it.                     |
| No third-party penetration test of SignuDoc itself.                                       | Commission one of your own install if your customers ask.                                              |
